Subject: Draft Council regulation (EEC) No.   /89 amending regulation
(EEC) No.1277/84 laying down general rules for the system of
production aid for processed fruit and vegetables.




The aim of the above-mentioned regulation is to introduce two
amendments to the regulation laying down general rules for the
system of production aid in this sector.


The first of these measures relates to the laying down of a maximum
celling for the levels of production aid which may be paid for tinned
peaches and pears,in order to ensure that the production aid is not
greater than the difference between the minimum price paid to the
community grower and the price paid by competing third country
processors for a comparable quality of raw material.


The second measure provides for the neutralIsing,by means of a system
of monetary adjustment,of the monetary gaps which may arise duing the
the course of a marketing year between the green rates and the market
rates of the various Community currencies.


The aim of this system is to re-establish an element of
competitiveness on Community markets and those of importing third
countr ies.
